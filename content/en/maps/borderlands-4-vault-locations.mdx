## Vault of Inceptus Location (The Fadefields)

The Vault of Inceptus serves as the foundational Primordial Arch in the vast, windswept plains of The Fadefields. 

### Step-by-Step Navigation Guide

1. **Secure the Keys**: Gather all three Fadefields Vault Key Fragments.
2. **Locate the Blue Indicator**: Open your world map and look at the **Dissected Plateau** sector.
3. **Select Fast Travel**: Teleport to either **The Stubs** safehouse or the **Synthetic Nave** checkpoint.
4. **Ascend the Vista**: Drive or sprint south toward the towering rock formation dominating the horizon.
5. **Activate the Console**: Ascend the winding natural rock ramp to the summit. Among the ruined Arch debris, interact with the stone console to deposit your three key fragments and breach the vault portal.

### Vault of Inceptus Route Details

| Landmark / Waypoint | Direction from Safehouse | Key Visual Cue | Hazard Warning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Stubs** | Starting Point (North) | Rebel outpost with vending stations | Roaming technicals |
| **Plateau Base** | South / Southeast | Wrecked industrial scaffolding | Heavy sniper nests |
| **Inceptus Summit** | Direct South (Climb Up) | Glowing purple Arch monoliths | Elite fauna spawns |

## Vault of Origo Location (Terminus Range)

Tucked away within the freezing peaks of the Terminus Range, the Vault of Origo demands cold-weather survival and careful cave exploration.

### Step-by-Step Navigation Guide

1. **Collect Key Fragments**: Clear the three fragment objectives across the Terminus Range.
2. **Fast Travel**: Warp directly to the **Heritage Opus Safehouse** located in the **Low Leys** region.
3. **Cross the Frozen Chasm**: Head northwest out of the safehouse, navigating the suspended bridge spanning the glacial ravine.
4. **Identify the Auger Shrine**: Push through the snowy blizzard until you see a luminous alien Auger Shrine marking a cavern opening.
5. **Enter the Ice Cavern**: Proceed through the cave entrance. The Arch portal chamber is situated deep within the cavern interior.

### Terminus Range Exploration Breakdown

| Route Stage | Landmark | Terrain Obstacle | Suggested Vehicle/Gear |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1** | Heritage Opus Safehouse | Deep snowbanks | Light Outrunner (Boost Heavy) |
| **Stage 2** | Low Leys Suspension Bridge | Narrow roadway, explosive barrels | On foot (Combat zone) |
| **Stage 3** | Auger Shrine Cave | Dark cavern, icy ledges | Jump-boost thrusters |

## Vault of Radix Location (Carcadia Burn)

The Vault of Radix is buried beneath the scorched sands and toxic ravines of Carcadia Burn. Finding this site requires navigating through a treacherous desert landscape.

### Step-by-Step Navigation Guide

1. **Acquire Fragments**: Pick up the three Carcadia Burn Vault Key Fragments across the zone.
2. **Fast Travel**: Teleport to the **Rustical Hurl Order Silo**.
3. **Cross the Canyon Bridge**: Spawn a vehicle and drive directly north over the wooden suspension bridge.
4. **Navigate the Wreckage**: Continue through the canyon until the road opens into a wide sandy bowl filled with scrap metal and alien debris.
5. **Summon the Arch**: Veer slightly northeast. As you approach the designated marker with all three fragments in your inventory, the entrance structure will rise directly out of the desert sands.

### Carcadia Burn Navigation Path

| Checkpoint | Action Required | Environmental Hazard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rustical Hurl Order Silo** | Spawn vehicle & restock ammo | Hostile turret towers |
| **Northern Canyon Bridge** | Cross at high speed | Destructible roadway sections |
| **The Grindstone Basin** | Disembark and approach center | Sandburrower ambush encounters |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I enter Borderlands 4 vault locations before finishing the main story?
Yes. As long as you have unlocked access to the overarching planetary zones (The Fadefields, Terminus Range, and Carcadia Burn) and collected the three regional Vault Key Fragments, you can open and conquer that specific Primordial Vault.

### What happens if I visit a vault location without the Key Fragments?
If you arrive at any of the **Borderlands 4 vault locations** without holding the required three fragments for that region, the activation altar will remain dormant, and the entrance portal or structure will not appear.

### Do bosses in the Primordial Vaults respawn for farming?
Yes. Once you complete a Primordial Vault questline, you can fast travel back to the entrance, step through the portal, and defeat the guardian boss repeatedly to farm their dedicated Legendary drop pool.

### How many total Vault Key Fragments are in Borderlands 4?
There are nine total Vault Key Fragments on Kairos—divided evenly into sets of three across The Fadefields, Terminus Range, and Carcadia Burn.

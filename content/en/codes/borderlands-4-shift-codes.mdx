To make sure your rewards show up in your mailbox without issues, you can redeem active promotions either directly in the game or via an external web browser.

### In-Game Redemption Process

1. Boot up *Borderlands 4* and load into the main menu or pause the active campaign.
2. Select the **SHiFT** tab from the menu bar.
3. Link your console or PC client (Steam, Epic Games Store, Xbox Network, PlayStation Network) to your official account.
4. Navigate to the **Rewards** tab, represented by the gift box icon.
5. Paste or type the 25-digit code into the entry field. Hyphens are formatted automatically, and text is not case-sensitive.
6. Click **Redeem** and confirm the loot in your **Reward History**.

If you do not have immediate console or PC access when temporary promos drop, you can register codes remotely through the [official Gearbox SHiFT Rewards portal](https://shift.gearboxsoftware.com/).

## Troubleshooting SHiFT Code Issues

Encountering an error message while redeeming **Borderlands 4 shift codes** can happen for a few common reasons:

```
Common SHiFT Errors:
├── "Code does not exist" -> Typo in character string (e.g., mistaking '8' for 'B' or '0' for 'O')
├── "Already redeemed"   -> Code used previously on linked platform profile
├── "Failed to communicate" -> Server downtime / Local connection interruption
└── "Expired code"       -> Exceeded promotional runtime window
```

1. **Check Character Strings**: SHiFT codes consist exclusively of alphanumeric characters. While dashes do not impact redemption on the website, accidental trailing spaces from copy-pasting often cause input failures.
2. **Platform Entitlements**: If you play on multiple systems (such as PC and PlayStation), verify you selected the correct target platform dropdown when submitting via browser.
3. **Verify Account Linkage**: If items do not populate in your in-game mailbox, visit the account settings inside the game menu to confirm your platform profile displays an active connection.

### Can I reuse the same SHiFT code across multiple characters?

### Where do I collect my gear after entering Borderlands 4 shift codes?
Once a code is successfully registered, cosmetics are unlocked directly inside your character customization station. Golden Keys are credited instantly to your wallet inventory and can be used immediately at any Golden Chest located in hub cities like Carcadia or The Launchpad.

### Do Golden Chest weapons scale with my character's level?
Yes. The loot generated by the Golden Chest matches the exact level of your active Vault Hunter at the moment you unlock the chest. For optimal efficiency, player experience suggests saving a portion of your keys until reaching endgame or tackling challenging boss difficulty spikes.

## S-Tier Builds: Dominating the Kairos Endgame

S-Tier configurations represent the absolute peak of the **Borderlands 4 build tier list**. These setups feature multiplicative damage scaling, infinite or rapid action skill uptime, and reliable life sustain against the toughest encounters in the game.

### 1. WYRMPUNCH Loveless (Malignant Construct)

The WYRMPUNCH setup for Loveless combines rapid melee triggers with the raw offensive power of the *Malignant Construct* action skill. By chaining key passives like *Giga Bite* and *barrag.exe*, players can trigger continuous status explosions that scale dynamically with level 70 gear. 

Community reports confirm that this setup trivializes UVH7 mobbing density while retaining massive single-hit burst damage against raid targets.

*   **Key Passives:** *Giga Bite*, *barrag.exe*, *Malignant Surge*
*   **Core Strengths:** Extremely high mobility, built-in shield gating, unmatched elemental scaling.
*   **Gear Focus:** Melee-enhanced shotguns, cooldown-reduction class mods, and splash damage relics.

### 2. B0tSh0t 2.0 C4SH (Cleromancy)

C4SH's *Cleromancy* tree holds the crown for the highest raw gun damage output in Borderlands 4. The B0tSh0t 2.0 build stacks luck-based and dice-roll damage modifiers via *Heavenly Sevens*, *Five is Alive*, and *Mighty Nine*. When properly chained, every critical hit triggers cascading bonus damage instances that shred bosses before their immune phases can even activate.

*   **Key Passives:** *Heavenly Sevens*, *Five is Alive*, *Mighty Nine*
*   **Core Strengths:** Best-in-class boss deletion, high critical hit forgiveness, deep magazine synergy.
*   **Gear Focus:** High-fire-rate multi-pellet pistols, Vladof assault rifles, and critical damage artifacts.

### 3. Grandmaster Rafa (Peacebreaker Cannons)

Rafa remains one of the safest and most lethal picks in solo play. The Grandmaster build relies on dual *Peacebreaker Cannons* to provide persistent automated fire while Rafa maneuvers around the battlefield. With full uptime on his ordnance buffs, Rafa effortlessly strips boss armor and health bars simultaneously.

## Gear Optimization and Progression Strategies for UVH7

Maximizing your placement on this **Borderlands 4 build tier list** requires syncing your skill tree synergies with appropriate endgame itemization. When farming legendary gear on Kairos, prioritize stat affixes according to the specific damage calculations of your build.

```
Target Weapon Farming Priority:
Base Damage Scaling > Multiplicative Skill Buffs > Action Skill Cooldown > Anointment / Part Modifiers
```

For updated patch notes, hotfixes, and balance updates that impact build effectiveness, consult the official [Borderlands website](https://borderlands.2k.com/) to keep your character optimized across current meta shifts.

### Essential Farming Checklist for Endgame Builds

1.  **Weapon Synergies:** Match your class mod passives (e.g., +3 to *Giga Bite* or +2 to *Heavenly Sevens*) with weapon archetypes that trigger those skills most frequently.
2.  **Shield Capacity vs. Delay:** Melee hybrid builds benefit from low-capacity shields that break quickly to trigger on-break damage buffs, whereas gun-focused Rafa builds require high-capacity shields with instant recharge gates.
3.  **Relic Scaling:** Ensure your relic secondary stats provide broad bonuses such as *Area-of-Effect Damage* or *Cooldown Rate* rather than narrow manufacturer-only boosts.

| Stat Priority Tier | Target Stat Attributes | Impact on UVH7 Performance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top Priority** | Action Skill Cooldown, Multiplicative Crit Damage | Reduces downtime, maximizes damage windows |
| **Secondary Priority** | Splash Damage Radius, Elemental Effect Chance | Enhances crowd clearing and status stacking |
| **Tertiary Priority** | Max Health, Reload Speed, Handling | Quality of life and minor defensive scaling |

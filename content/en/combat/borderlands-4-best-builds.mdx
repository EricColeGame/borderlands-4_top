## Combat Strategy: Tackling TUVH7 Mobs and Raid Bosses

To succeed in True Ultimate Vault Hunter Mode, player experience shows that mastering engagement pacing is just as vital as having top-tier gear.

1.  **Maintain Passive Buff Stacks**: Pre-fire or activate minor action skill triggers before walking into boss arenas to enter combat with full damage multipliers.
2.  **Match Elements to Enemy Health Bars**: Incendiary vs. Flesh, Shock vs. Shields, Corrosive vs. Armor, and Radiation/Cryo for crowd control and debuff application.
3.  **Manage Health Gating**: Keep survivability passives active so that incoming lethal hits leave you at low health rather than triggering Fight For Your Life (FFYL) immediately.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the Borderlands 4 best builds for solo bossing?
The highest single-target solo bossing setups currently are the **WYRMPUNCH Loveless** (Malignant Construct) and **B0tSh0t 2.0 C4SH** (Cleromancy). Both builds stack multiplicative critical and splash damage modifiers that can eliminate raid bosses in single damage windows.

### What is the current maximum level cap for endgame builds?
The Level 70 cap is the baseline for True Ultimate Vault Hunter (UVH7) difficulty, giving players enough skill points to reach the capstone passive of one tree while investing deeply into secondary and utility branches.

### How do I maintain survivability on high UVH7 difficulties?
Focus on passive skills that trigger lifesteal from gun or splash damage (such as Loveless's *Solid State Destruction* or C4SH's *Five is Alive* health-gate mechanics) paired with high-capacity ward shields to prevent sudden one-shot mechanics.

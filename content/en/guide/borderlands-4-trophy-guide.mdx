## Critical Missable Trophies & How to Avoid Missing Them

*Borderlands 4* features two missable trophies tied to specific dialogue choices and hidden mechanics during side missions. If you miss either, you can still start a boosted Level 30 character post-campaign or join a friend in co-op, but getting them right the first time saves significant backtracking.

### 1. "All Things Vend" (Side Mission: Vend of the Line)

During the climax of *Vend of the Line*, you face an eccentric vending machine boss named "Vend Zane." **Do not purchase anything immediately after beating the boss.**

| Step | Action Required | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Defeat Boss | Eliminate Vend Zane inside the cave encounter. |
| **2** | Inspect Clue | Look at the wooden board on the floor where Zane stood (shows 3 green crossed `$` and 1 orange `$`). |
| **3** | Input Sequence | Interact with the vending machine and perform: **SELL Green $\rightarrow$ SELL Green $\rightarrow$ SELL Green $\rightarrow$ BUY Legendary**. |
| **4** | Grab Item | Pick up the dropped blue-tier item: *"Picture of Zane and Levaine"*. |
| **5** | Turn In | Deliver the photo to Zane at Moxxi's Bottoms Up in Carcadia Burn to complete the alternate ending. |

### 2. "Rift Incompatible" (Side Mission: Exit Through the Rift Shop)

When confronting Francis Oddman at the conclusion of *Exit Through the Rift Shop*, you are presented with a choice to shoot or punch him.

* **Crucial Choice:** You must choose to **PUNCH** Francis Oddman so he gets launched into an interdimensional rift.
* **Trophy Unlock:** Afterward, travel to the Windspear area within the Terminus Range near the Dead Bolt collectible. You will discover Oddman encased in ice, popping the trophy immediately upon inspection.

The hardest individual trophy in your journey is **Gear Five!**, which requires you to push your game world to Ultimate Vault Hunter (UVH) Rank 5. After finishing the campaign, toggle UVH Mode via the session settings menu.

To climb the ranks, defeat specific open-world activity bosses (replayable at Moxxi's Big Encore stations), which unlocks a guaranteed Lilith Wildcard Mission. Completing the Wildcard mission permanently increases your rank.

## 4-Character Leveling & Vehicle Collection ("Catch-A-Ride")

Unlocking all 10 Personal Vehicles for the **Catch-A-Ride** trophy requires leveling all 4 launch Vault Hunter classes to Level 50. 

```
1. Siren (Vex)        ──> Challenge: "An Unseen Braid"   ──> Unlocks Skimmer
```

The first 6 vehicles unlock automatically as story quest rewards (including the *Digirunner* and *Driftblade*). The remaining four unlock via Character Challenges upon reaching Level 50.

3. **Characters 3 & 4 (Vault Grinders):** Start both classes at Level 30. Run repeated Primordial Vault arena clears on easy difficulty. Each run grants roughly one full level-up every 20–25 minutes.

## Miscellaneous & Secret Trophy Solutions

Several standalone trophies require interacting with specific world objects or spotting rare spawns across the map.

### Rare World Events & White Bubbles

* **Goredello (*Arena Grande*):** Random timed event located in *Terminus Range > Cuspid Climb*. Enter the battle ring and complete 3 waves before the timer expires.
* **Airship (*Reverse Abduction*):** Spawns in *The Fadefields > Hungering Plain > The Flarewell*. Get pulled into the tractor beam and destroy enemies across 4 successive airship waves.
* **Creep Impact (*Shoot the Moon*):** Located in *Carcadia Burn > Lopside > Stardown Point*. Complete the timed impact event when the blue marker appears.
* **World Boss Bubbles:** Rare large white spheres appearing in the open world. Step inside to trigger an elite boss encounter. *Caution: If you die or exit the bubble perimeter, the boss despawns immediately.*

### Secret Easter Eggs

* **Patricia Tannis Cutout (*Cut That Out*):** Head to the far southwestern coastline of *The Howl* in The Fadefields near the Coastal Bonescape boundary. Locate the wooden gazebo with colored explosive barrels. Shoot them in this exact order: **Green $\rightarrow$ Light Blue $\rightarrow$ Red $\rightarrow$ Dark Blue $\rightarrow$ Yellow**. A cutout of Dr. Tannis will emerge from the ocean.
* **Moxxi’s Tip Jar (*False Idolator*):** Walk up to the bar counter at Moxxi’s Bottoms Up and deposit either $100 or $1,000 into the tip jar to pop the trophy instantly.
* **Dave the Burrito (*Guac is Extra*):** Discover all 15 hidden Burrito collectibles scattered throughout the Dominion district, then interact with Dave in Upper Dominion.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I earn every trophy in this Borderlands 4 trophy guide on Easy difficulty?
Almost all trophies can be earned on Easy difficulty, including all 98 side missions, Primordial Vaults, and world activities. The only exception is the **Gear Five!** trophy, which requires you to complete boss encounters and Wildcard missions on Ultimate Vault Hunter Ranks 1 through 4 to unlock Rank 5.

### Are there any permanently missable trophies in Borderlands 4?
There are two missable trophies: *All Things Vend* (requiring a specific 3-green sell and 1-legendary buy sequence during "Vend of the Line") and *Rift Incompatible* (requiring you to punch Francis Oddman during "Exit Through the Rift Shop"). However, because you can create new Level 30 characters on post-story saves at any time, you can quickly replay these quests without restarting from Level 1.

### Do vehicle unlocks carry over across different characters?
Yes. All 10 Personal Vehicles and profile-level challenge unlocks are stored in your universal profile save. Once you level a Siren, Forgeknight, Gravitar, or Exo-Soldier to Level 50 to unlock their signature vehicle, that ride becomes immediately usable by all other current and future characters on your account.

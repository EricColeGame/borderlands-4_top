## Difficulty Settings and Modifiers

Before you fire your first shot on Kairos, you must select your starting difficulty. The game provides three distinct options that adjust enemy scaling, damage calculations, and loot drops. You can alter these settings at any time from the in-game gameplay menu if an encounter proves too demanding or you want better farming rates.

| Difficulty Mode | Incoming Damage | Cash Multiplier | Loot Quality Modifier | Elemental Matching Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Easy** | -20% Taken | Baseline (1.0x) | Standard | Minimal (Reduced penalties) |
| **Normal** | Baseline (1.0x) | Baseline (1.0x) | Standard | Standard (Balanced resistances) |
| **Hard** | +10% Taken | +10% Earned | Increased Drop Rates | Critical (Mismatches heavily penalized) |

### Which Difficulty Should You Choose?

* **Choose Easy** if you prefer focusing purely on the narrative, character interactions, and fast exploration without worrying about matching elemental affinities against tough shields and armor.
* **Choose Normal** if you want the classic Borderlands balance, offering steady combat pacing without extreme survival pressure.
* **Choose Hard** if you want top-tier Legendary loot earlier in your journey and enjoy min-maxing your build around elemental weaknesses.

## Pro Combat Tips for Boss Encounters

Boss battles on Kairos test both your DPS output and situational awareness. Keep these universal strategies in mind as you advance through the story:

* **Identify Multi-Bar Health Stages:** Many major bosses feature layered defenses (e.g., a massive blue Shield bar over a yellow Armor bar). Switch elements immediately as each bar depletes.
* **Keep Minions Alive for Second Winds:** Avoid wiping out weak adds immediately during boss fights. Leave them low on health so you can score an easy Second Wind revival if you enter "Fight For Your Life" mode.
* **Observe Environmental Hazards:** Boss arenas often feature explosive elemental barrels and jump pads. Use them to dodge heavy telegraphed AoE attacks and inflict free burst damage.
